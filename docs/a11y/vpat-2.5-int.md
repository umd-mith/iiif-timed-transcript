## Terms

The terms used in the Conformance Level information are defined as follows:

- **Supports:** The functionality of the product has at least one method that
  meets the criterion without known defects or meets with equivalent
  facilitation.
- **Partially Supports:** Some functionality of the product does not meet the
  criterion.
- **Does Not Support:** The majority of product functionality does not meet
  the criterion.
- **Not Applicable:** The criterion is not relevant to the product.
- **Not Evaluated:** The product has not been evaluated against the criterion.
  This can be used only in WCAG 2.x Level AAA.

### Declared deviation from VPAT 2.5

This report uses **Not Evaluated** on five Level A and AA criteria: **1.3.4,
1.4.4, 1.4.12, 2.5.2** and **4.1.2**. The template restricts that level to
Level AAA. The deviation is declared here rather than left to be discovered.

The permitted alternative is to assert one of the four scored levels on
evidence the author does not hold. At 4.1.2 that means asserting whether NVDA
and VoiceOver expose an open shadow root's accessibility tree, which source
inspection cannot establish and on which those two implementations have
historically differed.

The preferred remedy is to perform the outstanding evaluations and score the
rows, not to relabel them. The test scripts are written and committed. A
reviewer is entitled to reject the deviation.

## WCAG 2.1 Report

Conformance to WCAG 2.1 Success Criteria is reported for Level A and Level AA.
Level AAA is not included.

**Scoping note.** WCAG conformance is defined for a full page. This report
describes the component evaluated in isolation on a minimal host page.
Conformance of any page embedding it depends further on that page and on the
IIIF manifest supplied to it. Criteria the component cannot satisfy on its own
are marked Not Applicable with the reason stated in Remarks. Contrast claims
(1.4.3, 1.4.11) hold for shipped default `--iiif-player-*` token values only.

**Content responsibility note, applying to 1.2.1–1.2.5.** The component
renders whatever caption tracks and transcript annotations the supplied IIIF
manifest carries. It neither generates nor validates captions, transcripts, or
audio descriptions. Where a row reflects a gap in the component's own behavior
rather than in manifest content, Remarks say so. The component emits a
development-time console warning, numbered by success criterion, when a video
canvas carries no caption tracks (`src/lib/player/Viewer.svelte`).

**Host pages used for evaluation.** `examples/element/plain.html` carries a
deliberately hostile style block that remaps `--iiif-player-accent` and other
tokens, to prove they do not leak. It is the correct page for keyboard and
screen-reader work and the wrong page for any claim scoped to shipped
defaults. Default-appearance claims rest on
`docs/src/pages/canvas-nav-demo.astro`, which applies no `::part()` overrides
and no token remapping.
