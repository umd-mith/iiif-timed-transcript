IIIF Cookbook recipe 0219 (video + external VTT)

AVAnnotate export: embedded text with duplicate annotation ids

Local fixture (fixtures/avannotate-duplicate-ids.json), derived from a Scholarly Editing "Voices" interview: eight TextualBody segments that all carry the annotation page's id, the way an AVAnnotate export does. All eight must appear in the transcript panel — segments are not deduplicated by id. The audio is loaded cross-origin from scholarlyediting.org.

The manifest is served locally on purpose: scholarlyediting.org sends no Access-Control-Allow-Origin, so its manifests cannot be fetched from another origin.
